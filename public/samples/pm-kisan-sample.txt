GOVERNMENT OF INDIA
MINISTRY OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE
DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND FARMERS WELFARE

OPERATIONAL GUIDELINES
PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI (PM-KISAN)

1. BACKGROUND

In order to provide income support to all landholding farmers' families in the country to
enable them to take care of expenses related to agriculture and allied activities as well as
domestic needs, the Government has approved a new Central Sector Scheme, "Pradhan
Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)". The Scheme aims at supplementing the financial
needs of the farmers in procuring various inputs to ensure proper crop health and appropriate
yields, commensurate with the anticipated farm income.

2. SALIENT FEATURES OF THE SCHEME

Under the Scheme, an amount of Rs. 6,000/- per year is transferred in three four-monthly
instalments of Rs. 2,000/- each directly into the bank accounts of eligible farmer families,
subject to certain exclusion criteria relating to higher income status. The fund is transferred
directly to the bank accounts of the beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode.
All landholder farmer families, which have cultivable landholding in their names, are eligible
to get benefit under the scheme. e-KYC is mandatory for all beneficiaries to receive
instalments; beneficiaries can complete e-KYC on the PM-KISAN portal or at the nearest
Common Service Centre.

3. EXCLUSION CATEGORIES

The following categories of beneficiaries of higher economic status shall not be eligible for
benefit under the scheme:

(a) All Institutional Landholders are not eligible for benefit under the scheme.

(b) Any person who paid income tax in the last assessment year shall not be eligible to
    receive benefits under the scheme.

(c) All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government
    Ministries/Offices/Departments and its field units, Central or State Public Sector
    Undertakings and Attached offices/Autonomous Institutions under Government as well
    as regular employees of the Local Bodies (excepting Multi Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D
    employees) are not eligible for benefit under the scheme.

(d) Retired pensioners whose monthly pension is Rs. 10,000/- or more (excluding Multi
    Tasking Staff/Class IV/Group D employees) are not eligible for benefit under the scheme.

(e) Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants and Architects
    registered with Professional bodies and carrying out profession by undertaking practices
    are not eligible for benefit under the scheme.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

Farmer families wishing to avail benefit under the scheme should keep the following ready:
Aadhaar card of the farmer; land ownership documents/records of rights; bank account
details (bank account should be linked with Aadhaar for e-KYC).

5. PROCEDURE FOR REGISTRATION

State Governments/UT Administrations will carry out the identification of beneficiary farmer
families who are eligible for support as per scheme guidelines. Land ownership as on a cut-off
date shall be the basis for identifying beneficiaries. Farmers may also register themselves
through the PM-KISAN portal or approach their local Agriculture Officer or nearest Common
Service Centre with the documents listed above. After registration, farmers can track their
instalment status on the PM-KISAN website using their Aadhaar number.

6. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

For any queries or grievances, farmers may contact the PM-KISAN helpline at 155261 or write
to pmkisan-ict@gov.in. The scheme portal is available at pmkisan.gov.in.
